# com_biotime 1.3.6

Update from **System → Update → Extensions**, or install the attached package. No database changes.

**Manager Dashboard: choose up to a year.** From – To is no longer limited to 31 days: a manager
can look at a term or the whole school year, as on My Attendance.

Because every employee in the list is read for every day, a long period shows fewer employees at
a time, to keep the page and the PDF fast: up to 31 days 300 employees (as before), three months
about 100, a whole year 25. When more employees match, the page asks to choose a department or an
employee first, or a shorter period. The PDF of a long period is cut into several tables (on A3
one per month: a year is 12 pages).
